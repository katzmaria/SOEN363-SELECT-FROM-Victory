## How duplicates were made

- **Exact duplicate:** the original row repeated with the **same ID**, sometimes with one field's case or surrounding spaces changed (still identical after trimming and ignoring case). Placed somewhere after the original.
- **Near duplicate:** the same facts under a **new ID** (IDs continue after the last original ID), rendered again so dates, labels and phone numbers can use another format. They match the contract's duplicate keys: patient = name + DOB, staff = name + role, admission = patient + admit time, others = admission + timestamp + type. Placed at the end of the file.

## Source B conventions (for checking your profiling)

- **Timestamps** are local Africa/Kampala time. Usual format `DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM` (24h). Also present: ISO (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` or `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`), month-first with AM/PM (`MM/DD/YYYY hh:mm AM`), and a few dash dates (`NN-NN-YYYY HH:MM`) written in **either** order. Dash dates with a value over 12 can be read; dash dates where both numbers are 12 or less are the injected **ambiguous dates**. A slash date with AM/PM is always month-first; a 24h slash date is always day-first.
- **Date of birth**: usually `DD/MM/YYYY`; also ISO, `5 Mar 1980` style, and dash dates (same rule as above).
- **Temperature**: about 30% in Fahrenheit (`99.1F`, `99.1 F`, `99.1 °F`, or a bare `99.1`), the rest in Celsius (sometimes `37.2C`/`37.2 °C`). Other vitals sometimes carry units (`88 bpm`, `96%`, `4/10`, `120/80 mmHg`, `120-80`).
- **Missing-value placeholders** (injected): empty, `N/A`, `null`, `-`, `?`; `-1` for numbers; `0000-00-00` for dates. Values that are legitimately empty (e.g. no `TimeOut` for a current stay, no `RequestNo` for a walk-in exam, no findings for an exam not done, no `DischargeDateTime` for an active admission, no phone for young children) are written as an empty field and are **not** counted as problems.
- **Allergies / medications** with none: `none`, `None`, `NKA`, `no known allergies`, `No Known Allergies` (medications use `none`/`None`/`NKA`). These mean "none", not missing.
- **ICD-9 codes**: dictionary codes only, sometimes without the decimal point (`4019`), with extra spaces (` 428.0`), or `38.9` for `038.9`; plus fake codes from this list: `999.99`, `123.4`, `V99.9`, `000.1`, `E999.9`, `4O1.9`, `72.15`, `629.99`, `301.X`.
- **Invalid numbers** are only in optional triage vitals (e.g. temperature `300`, pulse `-72`, SpO2 `140`, pain `14`, `abc`). `-1` on its own is the missing-value placeholder, not an invalid number.
- **CSV**: UTF-8, comma-delimited, minimal quoting, CRLF line endings. Some clinical notes contain commas, double quotes and line breaks (quoted).
