> **Statement:** all of this data is entirely fictional, created exclusively for educational and learning purposes (SOEN 363 Project Phase 2). Names are random combinations of common Chinese, Malay and Indian names used in Singapore; addresses, phone numbers, insurance numbers and IDs are random.

All counts below are **records** (one XML element = one entity instance, nested ones included; the `<staff>`, `<patients>`, `<admissions>` and `<unlinkedRecords>` containers are not records). A record can have several problems, so the columns overlap (as section 7 of the contract allows). Every count comes from the script's own bookkeeping.

## How duplicates were made

- **Exact duplicate:** the same element again with the **same ID**, right after the original. A duplicated `<admission>` has no children of its own (the children stay under the first copy). In about 30% of them one text field differs only in case or spacing, so they are still exact duplicates under the contract.
- **Near duplicate:** the same facts under a **new ID** (numbering continues after the last original ID), written again so the date, labels and text can use another format or case. They match the contract's duplicate keys: patient = name + DOB, staff = name + role, admission = patient + admit time, others = admission + timestamp + type. For some near-duplicate patients and admissions, one child (an admission, or a note nested under the duplicate admission) points to the new ID, so the cleaning has to re-point it to the kept record (contract section 7).

## Source C conventions (for checking your profiling)

- **Structure:** root `<hospitalRecords>` (attributes: system, continent, timezone, extractTime) with `<staff>`, `<patients>`, `<admissions>` and `<unlinkedRecords>`. Inside each `<admission>`: `<triage>`, `<wardMovement>`, `<diagnosis>`, `<labOrder>` (with its `<imaging>` exams nested inside), `<imaging>` without an order (walk-in), `<operation>`, `<note type="...">` and `<discharge>`. The parent admission of a nested record is the enclosing `<admission>`; records in `<unlinkedRecords>` carry `admissionRef` instead, and an `<imaging orderRef="...">` directly under an admission points to an order that does not exist. A few records have no id attribute at all (malformed).
- **IDs:** patients `P000001`, admissions `E000001`, staff `S0001`, every other entity a plain number. The contract puts the numeric part in the key column and the full text ID in `source_record_id`.
- **Timestamps** are Asia/Singapore time (UTC+8, no DST). Usual format ISO 8601 with offset (`2024-05-08T14:59:00+08:00`). Also present: local time without offset (`2024-05-08 14:59`, still Singapore time), UTC with `Z` (`2024-05-08T06:59:00Z`), text dates (`08 May 2024 02:59 PM`), day-first slash dates (`08/05/2024 14:59`, always day-first), and a few dash dates (`NN-NN-YYYY HH:MM`) written in **either** order. Dash dates with a value over 12 can be read; dash dates where both numbers are 12 or less are the injected **ambiguous dates**.
- **Date of birth**: usually `YYYY-MM-DD`; also `DD/MM/YYYY`, `8 May 1980` style, and dash dates (same rule as above).
- **Names:** `<name><family/><given/></name>`. Chinese names are written family name first; Malay and Indian names use `bin`/`binte` and `s/o`/`d/o` with the father's name in `<family>`. Staff names are one `<name>` element.
- **Temperature**: `<temperature unit="C">37.2</temperature>`; about 30% in Fahrenheit (`unit="F"`, or no unit attribute and the unit in the text, `99.1F` / `99.1 °F`); some Celsius values carry the unit in the text (`37.2C`). **Oxygen saturation** is stored as a **fraction** (`0.97`), sometimes as a percent (`97%`). Other vitals sometimes carry units (`88 bpm`, `4/10`, `120/80 mmHg`, `120-80`).
- **Missing values** (injected): element left out, empty element, `N/A`, `null`, `-`, `?`; `-1` for numbers; `0000-00-00` for dates. Values that are legitimately empty (no `<dischargedAt>` for an active admission, no `<transferOut>` for a current unit stay, no `<report>` for an exam not done, no prescription or follow-up after a death) are left out and are **not** counted as problems.
- **Allergies / medications** with none: `none`, `None`, `NKA`, `NKDA`, `no known allergies`, `No Known Drug Allergy` (medications: `none`, `None`, `NKA`, `Nil`). These mean "none", not missing.
- **ICD-9 codes**: dictionary codes only, sometimes without the decimal point (`4019`), with extra spaces (` 428.0`), or `38.9` for `038.9`; plus fake codes from this list: `999.99`, `123.45`, `000.0`, `V99.9`, `888.8`.
- **Invalid numbers** are only in optional triage vitals (e.g. temperature `300`, pulse `-72`, SpO2 `1.40`, pain `14`, `abc`). `-1` on its own is the missing-value placeholder, not an invalid number.
