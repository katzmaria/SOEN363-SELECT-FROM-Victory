## How duplicates were made

- **Exact duplicate:** the same row again with the **same ID**, inserted a few lines after the original. In about 30% of them one text field differs only in case or spacing, so they are still exact duplicates under the contract ("every field is the same after trimming and ignoring case").
- **Near duplicate:** the same facts under a **new ID** (IDs continue after the last original ID), rendered again so dates, labels and text can use another format or case. They match the contract's duplicate keys: patient = name + DOB, staff = name + role, admission = patient + admit time, others = admission + timestamp + type. Placed at the end of the file. For some near-duplicate patients and admissions, one child row (an admission or a note) points to the new ID, so the cleaning has to re-point it to the kept record (contract section 7).

## Source A conventions (for checking your profiling)

- **Files:** one `.txt` file per entity in `data/raw/A_txt/`. UTF-8, `\n` line endings, **line 1 is a header**, fields separated by `|` with no quoting (a `|` inside a value was replaced by `/`). A few lines are malformed on purpose (wrong number of fields).
- **Timestamps** are local **Europe/Paris** wall-clock time (CET/CEST). Usual format `DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM` (24h). Also present: ISO (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` or `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`), French hour notation (`DD/MM/YYYY 14h59`), dotted dates (`DD.MM.YYYY HH:MM`, always day-first), and a few dash dates (`NN-NN-YYYY HH:MM`) written in **either** order. Dash dates with a value over 12 can be read; dash dates where both numbers are 12 or less are the injected **ambiguous dates**. Times inside the spring daylight-saving gap never occur; times in the autumn repeated hour (02:00-02:59 on the last Sunday of October) can occur and are ambiguous in UTC.
- **Date of birth**: usually `DD/MM/YYYY`; also ISO, French text dates (`5 mars 1980`), and dash dates (same rule as above).
- **Numbers** use a **decimal comma** (`37,2`). **Temperature**: about 30% in Fahrenheit (`99,1F`, `99,1 F`, `99,1 °F`, or a bare `99,1`), the rest in Celsius (sometimes `37,2C` / `37,2 °C`). Other vitals sometimes carry units (`88 bpm`, `96%`, `4/10`, `120/80 mmHg`, `120-80`). Pain is `eva` (0-10 scale).
- **Names:** `nom` is the family name, usually in capitals; `prenom` the first name. `full_name` = `prenom` + `nom`. Staff names are one field `Prenom NOM`.
- **Missing-value placeholders** (injected): empty, `N/A`, `null`, `-`, `?`; `-1` for numbers; `0000-00-00` for dates. Values that are legitimately empty (no `date_heure_sortie` for an active admission or a current unit stay, no `num_demande` for a walk-in exam, no `conclusion` for an exam not done, no prescription or appointment after a death) are written as an empty field and are **not** counted as problems.
- **Allergies / medications** with none: `none`, `None`, `NKA`, `no known allergies`, `No Known Allergies`, `aucune` (medications: `none`, `None`, `NKA`, `aucun`). These mean "none", not missing.
- **ICD-9 codes**: dictionary codes only, sometimes without the decimal point (`4019`), with extra spaces (` 428.0`), or `38.9` for `038.9`; plus fake codes from this list: `999.99`, `123.45`, `000.0`, `V99.9`, `888.8`.
- **Invalid numbers** are only in optional triage vitals (e.g. temperature `300`, pulse `-72`, SpO2 `140`, pain `14`, `abc`). `-1` on its own is the missing-value placeholder, not an invalid number.
- **Free text** (reasons, symptoms, notes, findings, follow-up advice) is written in English by this hospital; codes, labels and field names are French.
